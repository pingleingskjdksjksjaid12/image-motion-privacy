Image Motion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The short version

  • Everything you make stays on your phone or tablet.
  • We never see your photos, and we don’t know who you are.
  • There are no ads, no accounts and no chatting with strangers.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to save or share a loop.
  • There is one thing to buy, and the person who owns the Google account has to say yes to it.

Image Motion is a photo animation app made by Coolwood Design Studio. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Image Motion has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your photos, projects, settings or any other information to us or to anyone else.

Your photos and projects

Photos you open, and the projects you create from them, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device so you can carry on editing later. Delete a project in the app, or uninstall the app, and they are removed.

When you export a loop and choose Save to gallery, it is saved as an ordinary file in your device's Movies or Pictures folder. When you choose Share, the file goes to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Image Motion is free to use, with an optional one-off purchase that unlocks unlimited layers in exports. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; Image Motion never sees your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play, on your device, whether the unlock has been bought on your Google account.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only to tell you an export has finished while the app is in the background.
  • Keep the screen awake: only while an export is running.
  • Google Play billing: to offer and confirm the optional unlock.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app; Image Motion does not access the camera directly.

Children and families

Image Motion is suitable for all ages, including children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age: there are no accounts, no chat or sharing with other users, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing made in the app leaves the device unless the person using it chooses to save or share it.

The only purchase is the optional one-off unlock. Google Play asks the owner of the Google account to confirm it, and parents can also turn on purchase approval in Google Play’s family settings.

Exported loops are drawn fresh from the picture, so they do not carry hidden details such as where a photo was taken.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
